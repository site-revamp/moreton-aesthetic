# Moreton Aesthetics Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Moreton Aesthetics is a dentist-led medical aesthetics and advanced skincare clinic based in Ramsgreave, Blackburn, Lancashire. Founded by Dr Nabila Parekh, the clinic offers personalised facial aesthetics, skin rejuvenation and advanced skincare treatments, combining clinical expertise with modern aesthetic technology.

The redesigned homepage focuses on establishing accurate clinic information, simplifying treatment discovery and strengthening practitioner credibility to support customer enquiries and appointment bookings.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three key problems identified during the website review.

### 1. Corrected Clinic Location and Address
- Replaced outdated Stanley House Hotel & Spa location references with the current Ramsgreave clinic address.
- Updated the location presentation to reflect Moreton, Higher Ramsgreave Road, Ramsgreave, Blackburn, BB1 9DQ.
- Improved the consistency of location information to reduce customer confusion and support local SEO.

### 2. Improved Treatment Discovery and Navigation
- Organised the clinic's extensive treatment portfolio into clearer categories.
- Introduced more structured treatment summaries and direct navigation pathways.
- Made it easier for visitors to explore treatments such as injectables, skin rejuvenation, microneedling and advanced laser treatments.

### 3. Stronger Trust and Booking Presentation
- Increased the visibility of Dr Nabila Parekh's professional background and qualifications.
- Gave greater prominence to the clinic's award recognition and customer reviews.
- Strengthened consultation and appointment booking calls to action.
- Improved the presentation of credibility information to support informed booking decisions.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Established a more polished aesthetic aligned with the clinic's premium, dentist-led positioning.
- **Layout:** Created a clearer hierarchy across the hero, practitioner profile, treatment categories and trust-building sections.
- **User experience:** Simplified treatment discovery and improved access to appointment enquiries.
- **Navigation:** Structured treatment pathways to make the clinic's extensive service offering easier to explore.
- **Brand positioning:** Brought clinical expertise, personalised care and award recognition into the main homepage experience.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a more cohesive and professional presentation focused on customer confidence and conversion.

## 4. Implementation Notes

The redesign is a visual homepage concept. Before publication, all illustrative imagery should be replaced or verified against genuine clinic assets, and treatment links should be checked against the correct live URLs. The updated clinic address should also be verified across the website, footer, contact information and relevant structured data.
